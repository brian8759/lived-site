Apple Health

Lived uses optional Health access to create movement summaries. You can review or change Health access using Apple's system controls.

Photos

Lived supports the permission level iOS provides, including limited Photos access. With limited access, summaries may only reflect content iOS makes available to Lived.

Language

Lived supports English and Simplified Chinese. On supported versions of iOS, you can choose Lived's language in the app-specific system settings.

Your data

Lived is designed as a local-first app. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Apple 健康

Lived 可选择使用“健康”权限来生成活动摘要。你可以通过 Apple 的系统控制查看或修改健康权限。

照片

Lived 支持 iOS 提供的照片权限级别，包括“有限访问”。在有限访问状态下，摘要可能只反映 iOS 向 Lived 开放的内容。

语言

Lived 支持英语和简体中文。在支持的 iOS 版本上，你可以在 Lived 的 App 专属系统设置中选择语言。

你的数据

Lived 采用 local-first（设备优先）设计。详情请阅读隐私政策。
