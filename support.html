Common questions

Why does Lived ask for Health access?

Some Lived experiences use Apple Health information for daily views, summaries, or receipts. You control permissions in Apple’s Health/privacy settings.

Why does Lived ask for Photos access?

Photo access supports photo-related experiences. You control it through iOS Settings; restricted or revoked access can make related features unavailable or incomplete.

Does Lived send my Health data, reflections, or receipt text to Firebase?

The current production telemetry design is intended not to send raw HealthKit measurements, reflection or journal text, or full receipt contents to Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics. Analytics uses coarse product events and Crashlytics handles crashes and non-fatal diagnostics.

Does Lived use advertising tracking?

No. The current production build does not use IDFA for advertising tracking, does not link AdSupport for analytics, and does not use Firebase Performance Monitoring.

How do I change privacy settings or delete local data?

Review the available privacy/telemetry controls in Lived Settings, manage Health and Photos permissions in iOS, use available in-app deletion/reset controls, or remove the app. Removing Lived may remove local app data, subject to iOS and device-backup behavior.

A receipt is missing, incomplete, or the app crashed

Receipts can depend on permissions, available Health data, Photos access, local processing, and app version. Try reopening the app. If a problem continues, email support with your app version, iOS version, device model, screen, and reproduction steps.
