Privacy Policy

Lived · Updated September 2026

Lived is designed to help you create a personal record of your day while keeping sensitive personal content on your device whenever possible. This policy explains how Lived handles information used by the app.

Information Lived uses

Apple Health

If you choose to allow Health access, Lived may read the Health data needed to create your daily movement summary, such as steps, walking or running distance, and workouts. Lived uses this information to present day and receipt summaries.

Lived does not sell Health data or use Health data for advertising, marketing, or data brokerage.

Photos

If you choose to allow Photos access, Lived may use information available through your Photos permission to summarize photos and videos associated with your day. If you grant limited Photos access, Lived can only work with the content iOS makes available to the app.

Lived is designed so that your photo and video library content is not automatically uploaded to a Lived-operated server for these daily summaries.

Reflections and receipts

Text you enter as a reflection and the daily receipts created from your information are designed to be stored and processed locally by the app. Lived does not sell your reflections.

On-device processing

The core Lived experience is designed to be local-first. Health information, photo-library information used for daily summaries, reflection text, and receipt rendering are intended to be processed on your device rather than sent to a Lived-operated cloud service.

Sharing

Lived may let you choose to share a receipt using the iOS share sheet. Sharing happens only when you initiate it. Once you share content with another app or service, that recipient's privacy practices apply.

Permissions and your choices

You can decline optional Health or Photos access and can review or change those permissions using Apple's system settings. Some Lived summaries may be incomplete or unavailable when access is limited or turned off.

Data retention

Data stored locally by Lived remains subject to your device, app, and backup settings. Removing the app may remove locally stored app data. Device and iCloud backups, if enabled, are controlled by your Apple settings and Apple's services.

Diagnostics

Apple may provide developers with crash or diagnostic information depending on your device settings and Apple's terms. Lived should not include your reflection text, Health data, or photo/video content in application logs or analytics.

Changes to this policy

If Lived's data practices materially change, this policy will be updated before or with the relevant app release.

Lived 的设计目标，是帮助你记录真实生活中的每一天，并尽可能将敏感的个人内容保留在你的设备上。本政策说明 Lived 如何处理 App 使用过程中涉及的信息。

Lived 使用的信息

Apple 健康

如果你选择允许访问“健康”，Lived 可能会读取生成每日活动摘要所需的健康数据，例如步数、步行或跑步距离以及锻炼记录。Lived 使用这些信息生成每日页面和回执中的活动摘要。

Lived 不会出售健康数据，也不会将健康数据用于广告、营销或数据经纪。

照片

如果你选择允许访问“照片”，Lived 可能会使用你授予的照片权限范围内可用的信息，对当天相关的照片和视频进行摘要。如果你只授予“有限访问”，Lived 只能使用 iOS 向本 App 开放的内容。

Lived 的设计不会为了生成这些每日摘要，而自动把你的照片或视频内容上传到由 Lived 运营的服务器。

文字记录与每日回执

你输入的文字记录，以及基于这些信息生成的每日回执，设计上由 App 在本地存储和处理。Lived 不会出售你的文字记录。

设备端处理

Lived 的核心体验采用 local-first（设备优先）设计。用于每日摘要的健康信息、照片资料库信息、你的文字记录以及回执渲染，均应在你的设备上处理，而不是发送到由 Lived 运营的云端服务。

分享

Lived 可以让你主动通过 iOS 的系统分享菜单分享每日回执。只有在你主动发起分享时，内容才会被发送给你选择的其他 App 或服务。分享之后，该接收方自己的隐私政策将适用于其处理的内容。

权限与选择

你可以拒绝可选的“健康”或“照片”权限，也可以通过 Apple 的系统设置查看或修改这些权限。当权限受到限制或被关闭时，部分 Lived 摘要可能不完整或无法使用。

数据保留

Lived 在本地保存的数据受你的设备、App 和备份设置影响。删除 App 可能会删除本地保存的 App 数据。如果你启用了设备或 iCloud 备份，其处理方式由你的 Apple 设置和 Apple 的服务决定。

诊断信息

根据你的设备设置和 Apple 的相关条款，Apple 可能向开发者提供崩溃或诊断信息。Lived 不应把你的文字记录、健康数据或照片/视频内容写入应用日志或分析系统。

政策更新

如果 Lived 的数据处理方式发生重大变化，我们会在相关版本发布之前或同时更新本隐私政策。
