Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated September 21, 2026

This policy explains how the Lived iOS application handles information.

Privacy at a glance

  • HealthKit is accessed only after permission and is used for Lived’s daily-life and wellness-related features.
  • Raw HealthKit measurements, photo contents and identifiers, reflection text, and full receipt contents are not intentionally sent to Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics.
  • Lived uses Firebase Analytics for limited product analytics and Firebase Crashlytics for crash and non-fatal diagnostics.
  • Lived uses Apple-native MetricKit and OSLog/signposts for performance and reliability instrumentation.
  • The current production build does not use Firebase Performance Monitoring, does not link AdSupport for analytics, and does not use IDFA for advertising tracking.
  • Lived does not sell HealthKit information.

Information Lived accesses on your device

HealthKit

If you grant Health permissions, Lived may read HealthKit information needed for daily experiences, summaries, insights, or receipts. The available information depends on your permissions, Apple Health data, and enabled app features. HealthKit information is used to provide functionality directly to you—not for advertising, marketing, cross-app tracking, or advertising-related profiling.

Photos

If you grant Photos access, Lived may access photos and related information needed for photo-related features. Lived does not intentionally send photo contents, filenames, PhotoKit local identifiers, or PHAsset identifiers to Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics.

Reflections, receipts, and other user content

Some content is stored locally on your device as needed for Lived to work. Lived’s telemetry boundary is designed not to send reflection or journal text, full receipt contents, raw HealthKit measurements, photo contents or identifiers, contact information entered as free-form content, precise location, filesystem paths, secrets, tokens, or API keys to Firebase Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics.

Analytics and diagnostics

Firebase Analytics

Lived uses Google Analytics for Firebase for limited, structured product events, such as onboarding and permission flows, Today and Settings views, daily processing results, receipt activity, reflection workflow state, and coarse app/version/build context. Events intentionally use coarse product information rather than sensitive content. Where available in the shipping version, in-app privacy controls manage future analytics collection.

Firebase Crashlytics

Crashlytics may process crash stack traces, application state around a crash, device and operating-system details, app version/build, coarse workflow context, developer-defined non-fatal errors, and Analytics breadcrumbs when enabled. Lived’s diagnostics layer is designed not to deliberately attach sensitive content. Where available in the shipping version, in-app controls manage future diagnostic collection.

MetricKit and OSLog

Lived uses Apple-native instrumentation for launch performance, hangs, responsiveness, memory, CPU, energy, disk/resource behavior, and important workflows. It does not intentionally place raw HealthKit data, photo contents, reflection text, or full receipt contents into OSLog/signpost metadata.

Your choices, retention, and security

You can manage Health permissions through Apple’s Health/privacy settings, Photos permissions through iOS Settings, available Lived privacy controls, and local content through app controls or app removal. Locally stored information is retained as needed for app functionality and subject to device-backup behavior. Firebase information is retained and processed under the applicable Firebase configuration, service terms, and retention controls. We use reasonable measures intended to reduce unauthorized access, but no storage or transmission method is completely secure.

International processing, children, and changes

Apple, Google, and their service providers may process information in other countries under their applicable terms, privacy documentation, and legal obligations. Lived is intended to be used consistently with applicable child-privacy laws and its App Store age rating. We may update this policy when data practices, features, service providers, or legal obligations change.
